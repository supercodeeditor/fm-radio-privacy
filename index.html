Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Phoenix Global Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM mobile application (Package ID: com.hifiradio.fmradio, hereinafter referred to as the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, sharing, and protection of user data when you use our App, in full compliance with Google Play's User Data and Data Safety policies.

Privacy-First & Offline-First Core Architecture: The App does not require user account registration, does not maintain any developer-owned cloud user database, and processes all 32-band HiFi DSP audio rendering, real-time AI speech recognition subtitles, and bilingual translations 100% locally on your device. However, integrated third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Google Play Services, and Google ML Kit) automatically collect and share Device or other IDs (such as Advertising ID and Android ID) over encrypted connections as detailed in Section 3 below.

1. Summary of Information Collection and Use

  • No Direct Personal Identity Accounts: We do not ask you to register an account, nor do we directly collect your real name, email address (unless you voluntarily email us for support), physical mailing address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS coordinates.
  • Automated SDK Data Collection & Sharing (Device or other IDs): To provide free ad-supported radio streaming, in-app VIP subscriptions, Google Cast streaming, and on-device AI model delivery, third-party SDKs integrated into the App collect and share Device or other IDs (including Google Advertising ID / AAID, Android ID, App Set ID, and Firebase/Play Services Installation IDs), App activity (in-app interactions and ad impressions/clicks), and App info and performance (crash logs and diagnostics).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted off the device by the App and its integrated SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.

3. Third-Party SDKs, Advertising & Subscriptions

To support ongoing audio engineering and offer optional ad-free VIP upgrades, the App integrates the following certified third-party services:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP) (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads): For non-VIP users, we use Google AdMob to display banners, app-open ads, and optional rewarded ads (to unlock 20-minute AI subtitle sessions). AdMob collects and shares Device or other IDs (specifically the Google Advertising ID / AAID and App Set ID), coarse IP-based location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, perform analytics, and combat ad fraud. In the EEA, UK, and applicable regions, consent is managed via Google UMP (GDPR / CCPA compliant). The App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission in its manifest for this purpose.
  • Google Play Billing V8 (com.android.billingclient:billing): Used for optional VIP subscriptions and purchase restorations. Google Play processes all payment transactions and uses device/account identifiers for fraud prevention and subscription entitlement verification. Phoenix Global Studios never collects or accesses your credit card or bank details.
  • Google ML Kit On-Device Translation (com.google.mlkit:translate): Real-time bilingual subtitle translation runs locally on your device. However, when the ML Kit SDK downloads ~30 MB offline neural translation models from Google servers or checks model integrity, its internal telemetry component (datatransport) transmits Device or other IDs (installation/device identifiers) and download diagnostics to Google for app functionality and reliability analysis.
  • Google Cast Framework (com.google.android.gms:play-services-cast-framework) & Play Core (In-App Updates, In-App Review, Play Asset Delivery): Used to discover Chromecast devices, deliver the on-demand Vosk acoustic asset pack (vosk_model_asset), check for app updates, and provide the native Google Play review dialog. These Google Play Services libraries transmit device/installation identifiers to Google to ensure secure delivery and quota compliance.
  • Public Radio Directories & Community Lyrics (Radio-Browser & LRCLIB): The App queries public radio directories (radio-browser.info) and the open community lyrics service (lrclib.net) over HTTPS. When auto-matching lyrics for local audio tracks, an on-device 6-layer PII privacy firewall strictly blocks personal voice recordings and strips file paths before sending only the sanitized song title and artist name.

You can review Google's official privacy policy and data practices here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. On-Device AI Subtitles & Local Lossless Media

  • On-Device AI Speech Recognition (Real-Time CC Subtitles): When you enable real-time English subtitles, audio streams are decoded locally in your device's volatile memory (RAM) using the embedded offline Vosk engine (vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15). We never record, store, or upload any radio audio or voice streams to any external server.
  • Local Lossless Audio & Lyrics (SAF): When you import local audio files (FLAC, WAV, ALAC, APE, MP3, M4A, OGG) or .lrc files, access is granted exclusively by you via Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF). Your personal audio files never leave your device.

5. System Permissions, Local Casting & Foreground Services

The App requests specific Android permissions strictly necessary for audio streaming and device casting:

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to access the Google Advertising ID for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention (unless disabled by VIP subscription or user opt-out).
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream online radio broadcasts, download on-demand AI models, and load station artwork.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK & WAKE_LOCK: Used exclusively to keep HiFi audio playing smoothly when your screen is locked, in the background, or connected to Android Auto.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Used exclusively to discover UPnP/DLNA smart TVs and Google Cast speakers on your local Wi-Fi network (LAN). When casting local audio or relaying streams, the App runs a temporary local-only HTTP proxy server on your phone's LAN IP.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & com.android.vending.BILLING: Used for media playback controls in the notification bar and Google Play VIP subscription billing.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & Resetting Device IDs

  • Local App Preferences & Crash Blackbox: Favorites, playback history, custom 32-band EQ presets, and local crash diagnostic logs (which are only shared if you manually choose to send an email via your mail client) are stored 100% locally on your device. You can permanently delete all local data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the App.
  • Deleting or Resetting "Device or other IDs" (Advertising ID): Because our App does not maintain user accounts or developer-controlled cloud servers, we do not store your Device IDs on our own servers. However, you have full control over the Google Advertising ID (AAID) collected and shared by Google SDKs. You can reset or permanently delete your Advertising ID at any time on your Android device by navigating to:
    Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some Android devices).
  • Data Retention by Third-Party SDKs: Data collected by Google AdMob, Google Play Services, and Google ML Kit is retained and deleted in accordance with Google's Data Retention Policies.

8. Children's Privacy

9. Security

We safeguard all off-device network and SDK traffic using encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols and enforce strict local sandbox isolation for all on-device C++ DSP and AI speech processing.
