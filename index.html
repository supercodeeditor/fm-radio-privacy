Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Phoenix Global Studios operates the FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize your privacy. The App does not require account registration, nor does it collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the App free, we use third-party advertising services (e.g., Google AdMob). These third-party services may collect non-sensitive device diagnostics, location indicators, or Google Advertising ID (AAID) to serve tailored ads and prevent fraud in accordance with their privacy policies.

3. Permissions & Foreground Services

The App requests specific Android system permissions solely to deliver core radio streaming functionalities:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to establish connection to online audio streams.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used exclusively to ensure continuous audio playback when the user closes the screen or runs the App in the background.
  • WIFI_STATE: Used to discover local network devices for casting (DLNA / Google Cast).

4. Audio Content & DMCA Disclaimer

FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM is an audio streaming aggregator. The audio streams made available through the App are hosted on public third-party servers across the internet. We do not host, store, or broadcast any media files directly.

All trademarks, copyright material, and broadcast streams belong to their respective owners. If you believe your copyrighted content is being infringed upon without authorization, please contact the original stream provider or reach out to us for stream link removal.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us non-personal access, and we endeavor to use commercially acceptable means of protecting network traffic using encrypted protocols where available.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at phoenixglobalstudios@gmail.com.
