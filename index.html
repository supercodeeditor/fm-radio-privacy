Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Phoenix Global Studios operates the FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM mobile application (Package ID: com.hifiradio.fmradio). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Privacy-First & Offline-First Architecture: The App does not require account registration, does not maintain any cloud user database, and processes all 32-band HiFi DSP audio rendering, real-time AI subtitles, and bilingual translations 100% locally on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize your privacy. The App does not require you to create an account, nor does it collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location on any external server.

2. On-Device AI Subtitles, Offline Translation & Local Media

Our App includes advanced audio and artificial intelligence features designed to run strictly on your device:

  • On-Device AI Speech Recognition (Real-Time CC Subtitles): When you enable real-time subtitles for radio broadcasts, audio streams are processed locally in your device's volatile memory (RAM) using an embedded offline acoustic engine (Vosk). We never record, store, or upload any audio streams or voice data to any cloud server.
  • On-Device Neural Translation (Google ML Kit): Real-time bilingual subtitle translation is performed locally on your device using offline translation models. Downloaded language models are stored strictly within the App's local sandbox.
  • Local Lossless Audio & Lyrics (SAF): When you choose to play local audio files (such as FLAC, WAV, MP3, AAC, or OGG) or import .lrc lyrics from your device, file access is granted explicitly by you through Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF). We do not upload or index your personal media files on external servers. When matching lyrics for local tracks, only the song title and artist metadata are queried against public lyrics databases.

3. Third-Party Services, Advertising & Subscriptions

To support ongoing development and offer optional ad-free VIP upgrades, the App integrates certified third-party services provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): For free-tier users, we use Google AdMob to display banners, app-open ads, and optional rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect non-sensitive device diagnostics, coarse IP-based location indicators, and your Google Advertising ID (AAID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud, strictly in accordance with your consent choices managed via Google UMP (GDPR / CCPA compliant). You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Google Play Billing (VIP Subscriptions): All in-app VIP subscription purchases, renewals, and restorations are processed securely and directly by Google Play. Phoenix Global Studios never collects, processes, or has access to your credit card number, bank account, or billing details.
  • Google Play Core (In-App Updates & Asset Delivery): Used to check for official App updates and deliver offline AI language packages securely via Google Play.

You can review Google's official privacy practices here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Permissions, Local Casting & Foreground Services

The App requests specific Android system permissions solely to deliver core HiFi audio streaming, casting, and automotive functionalities:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to public online radio streams, fetch station metadata, and load album artwork.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used exclusively to ensure uninterrupted HiFi audio playback when you lock your screen, switch apps, or connect to Android Auto.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Used exclusively to discover smart TVs, UPnP/DLNA renderers, and Google Cast (Chromecast) speakers on your local Wi-Fi network (LAN). When casting local audio or incompatible radio streams, the App runs a temporary, local-only HTTP proxy server on your phone's LAN IP to relay the audio stream directly to your TV or speaker. No local network data ever leaves your home router.
  • Android Auto Integration: Allows safe, distraction-free station browsing and playback control through your vehicle's head unit display.

5. Audio Content & DMCA Disclaimer

FM Radio: HiFi Radio AM FM is an audio streaming player and directory aggregator. The audio streams made available through the App are hosted on public third-party servers across the internet. We do not host, store, or broadcast any third-party radio media files directly.

All station names, logos, trademarks, copyright material, and broadcast streams belong to their respective owners. If you are a copyright owner or broadcaster and believe your content is being linked without authorization, please contact the original stream provider or reach out to us at phoenixglobalstudios@gmail.com for prompt station link removal.

6. Local Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

All user preferences—including your favorite stations, custom 32-band EQ acoustic presets, theme selections, and recent playback snapshots—are stored 100% locally on your device (via Android Room database and SharedPreferences). Because we do not store personal data on external servers, you have complete control over your data and can permanently delete all stored preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Security

We value your trust in using our App. We safeguard network traffic using encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols where supported by stream providers, and enforce strict local sandbox isolation for all on-device DSP and AI processing.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at: phoenixglobalstudios@gmail.com
